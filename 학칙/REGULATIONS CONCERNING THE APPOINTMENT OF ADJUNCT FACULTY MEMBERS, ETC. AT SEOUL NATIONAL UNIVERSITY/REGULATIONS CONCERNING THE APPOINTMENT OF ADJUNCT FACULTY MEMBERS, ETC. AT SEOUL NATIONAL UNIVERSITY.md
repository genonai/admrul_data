# REGULATIONS CONCERNING THE APPOINTMENT OF ADJUNCT FACULTY MEMBERS, ETC. AT SEOUL NATIONAL UNIVERSITY
> 학교규정 · 학칙 · 시행 2019-08-01 · 공포 2019-06-21 · 전부개정

### 전문
CHAPTER I GENERAL PROVISIONSArticle 1 (Purpose)The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe matters necessary for the appointment and personnel management of adjunct faculty members and other faculty members (hereinafter referred to as “non-tenure-track faculty members”) pursuant to Article 17 of the Higher Education Act, Article 7 of the Enforcement Decree of the same Act, and Article 31 of the Articles of Incorporation of the National University Corporation, Seoul National University.Article 2 (Definitions of Terms)(1) The term “non-tenure-track faculty member” in these Regulations means any of the following persons:1. The term “adjunct faculty member” means a person appointed on a part-time basis to lecture on a subject requiring practical field experience or to conduct collaborative research, etc., because the details of his or her duties are suitable for education and research which he or she will be in charge of at Seoul National University, who is a teacher holding dual office referred to in Article 7 of the Enforcement Decree of the Higher Education Act;2. The term “visiting faculty member” means a person appointed on a full-time basis to perform educational or research activities as prescribed in the relevant contract, who is an invited teacher defined in Article 7 of the Enforcement Decree of the Higher Education Act;3. The term “teaching faculty member” means a person appointed on a full-time basis to take exclusive charge of lectures as prescribed in the relevant contract, who is an invited teacher defined in Article 7 of the Enforcement Decree of the Higher Education Act;4. The term “invited faculty member” means a person appointed on a part-time basis to perform educational or research activities, who has attained outstanding academic and research achievements at home and abroad or has greatly contributed to national and social development;5. The term “clinical faculty member” means an expert in the clinical field, who is appointed on a full-time basis to perform activities concerning clinical education, training, treatment, or research or who belongs to a corporate hospital related to Seoul National University (including a hospital operated by Seoul National University on consignment) and is appointed on a part-time basis by Seoul National University;6. The term “industry-academia cooperation faculty member” means a person appointed on a full-time basis to take exclusive charge of affairs concerning industry-academia cooperation, such as lectures and technology transfer;7. The term “guest faculty member” means a person appointed on a full-time or part-time basis to perform educational or research activities, who has practical experience in a state agency, educational institution, research institute, public organization, industrial enterprise, etc. and has attained academic achievements;8. The term “clinical instructor” means an expert in the clinical field who is appointed on a part-time basis to perform educational or research activities;9. The term “research faculty member” means a person appointed on a full-time basis to perform research activities, etc. in and outside Seoul National University.(2) The term “specific subject” in these Regulations means a subject separately determined by a college or graduate school, the College of Liberal Studies, or the Faculty of Liberal Education according to the characteristics of curricula.(3) The term “institution to which a faculty member belongs” means a college or graduate school, the College of Liberal Studies, the Faculty of Liberal Education, a research facility, an affiliated facility, etc.Article 3 (Scope of Application)Except as otherwise provided in other Regulations, these Regulations shall apply to the appointment of non-tenure-track faculty members.Article 4 (Timing and Period of Appointment)(1) A non-tenure-track faculty member shall be, in principle, appointed on the first day of each month.(2) The period of appointment for a non-tenure-track faculty member shall be at least one year and not more than three years. However, the period of appointment may be less than one year in any of the following cases:1. Where a non-tenure-track faculty member is needed to urgently replace a faculty member for the remainder of a semester because the faculty member takes sick leave, maternity leave, or leave of absence, is dispatched, receives disciplinary action, or utilizes a research year (not more than six months) for less than six months during the semester or the faculty member is released from his or her position, retires from office, or is dismissed during the semester;2. Where a non-tenure-track faculty member is appointed for less than one year of research or participation in industry-academia cooperation which is commissioned by an institution other than a school referred to in Article 2 of the Higher Education Act;3. Where a person who works, as a permanent employee, for the State or a local government (excluding national and public schools under Article 3 of the Higher Education Act) or for a public institution under Article 4 of the Act on the Management of Public Institutions is appointed as a non-tenure-track faculty member for the efficient operation of curricula or classes.(3) A non-tenure-track faculty member shall be, in principle, reappointed for a period not exceeding three years in total, including the period of his or her new appointment, except in extenuating circumstances. This stipulation may not apply after evaluation has been conducted and the approval of the President has been obtained.(4) A person eligible for appointment as a non-tenure-track faculty member shall be, in principle, 65 years of age or younger. However, this stipulation may not apply if deemed particularly necessary by the President.Article 5 (Procedures for Appointment)(1) A non-tenure-track faculty member shall be appointed by the President upon the recommendation of the head of the institution to which he or she will belong after deliberation by the Faculty Personnel Committee. However, in the case of an affiliated facility or research facility supervised by a college or graduate school, such recommendation shall be made by the dean of the supervising college or graduate school.(2) The new employment of a non-tenure-track faculty member shall be, in principle, open employment, and, in cases falling under Article 4 (2) 1, a non-tenure-track faculty member may be specially employed.(3) Detailed procedures and specific evaluation standards, items, weights, and methods necessary for examining the new employment and reappointment of a non-tenure-track faculty member, and the composition and commission of examiners and other relevant matters shall be separately determined by the head of the institution to which the faculty member will belong or belongs.(4) In appointing a non-tenure-track faculty member, the President shall make such appointment after determining working conditions, including the following matters:1. Period of appointment;2. Lecture hours (where the faculty member will be in charge of lectures);3. Wages and the method of payment for this purpose ;4. Working conditions including service;5. Grounds for dismissal;6. Other matters that need to be determined by a contract.(5) A contract for appointment under paragraph (4) shall be entered into in writing and shall be in the attached Form in principle.Article 6 (Grounds for Disqualification for Appointment)No person falling under any subparagraph of Article 19 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines of Seoul National University (hereinafter referred to as the “Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines”) shall be appointed as a non-tenure-track faculty member.Article 7 (Personnel Records)The President shall prepare, maintain, and keep personnel records for efficient personnel management of non-tenure-track faculty members.Article 8 (Required Documents)In recommending the appointment of a non-tenure-track faculty member, the following documents shall be submitted:1. A letter of recommendation for appointment;2. A plan for utilization;3. A personnel record card or curriculum vitae (including research outcomes);4. A certificate of graduation and a diploma, and a certificate of career (however, these certificates may be replaced by documents verifying the qualifications);5. A written reply to a request for inquiry into sex offense history;6. A written consent document by the head of the institution to which the relevant faculty member originally belongs and the faculty member himself or herself (limited to applicable persons);7. A copy of the minutes of the personnel committee (or steering committee for personnel management) of the institution to which the relevant faculty member belongs or the supervising college or graduate school;8. A copy of a visa suitable for the status of stay granted pursuant to the Immigration Act after obtaining approval of the President (limited to non-Korean nationals);9. Other documents necessary for appointment.Article 9 (Contractual Details for Appointment and Service)(1) Contractual details for appointment of a non-tenure-track faculty member may be determined differently for each faculty member.(2) Various matters concerning the service of a full-time non-tenure-track faculty member, such as providing lectures and holding dual offices, shall be separately determined by the head of the institution to which he or she belongs.Article 10 (Mandatory Retirement)Where a non-tenure-track faculty member falls under any of the following cases, he or she shall mandatorily retire:1. Where he or she dies;2. Where the period of his or her appointment expires;3. Where he or she falls under Article 19 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines. However, subparagraph 2 of the same Article shall apply only where a person declared bankrupt has failed to file an application for immunity by the deadline for application, or a decision not to grant immunity to him or her or the revocation of his or her immunity has become final and conclusive in accordance with the Debtor Rehabilitation and Bankruptcy Act, and subparagraph 5 of the same Article shall apply only where a person, who committed a crime referred to in Articles 129 through 132 or 303 of the Criminal Act or Article 10 of the Act on Special Cases concerning the Punishment of Sexual Crimes, or a crime referred to in Article 355 or 356 of the Criminal Act in relation to his or her duties, has been convicted or suspended of the sentence of his or her imprisonment without labor or a heavier punishment by a court.Article 11 (Dismissal)Where a non-tenure-track faculty member falls under any of the following cases, the President may dismiss him or her on the recommendation of the head of the institution to which he or she belongs:1. Where he or she violates his or her appointment contract or fails to faithfully fulfill the contract;2. Where his or her dismissal is deemed unavoidable due to such reasons as the abolition of the institution to which he or she belongs;3. Where a research task, project, etc. in which he or she participates is completed;4. Where he or she degrades the reputation of Seoul National University;5. Where it is difficult for him or her to perform his or her duties due to illness or other reasons.Article 12 (Provision of Identification Card and Convenient Access to Facilities)(1) An identification card shall be issued to a non-tenure-track faculty member.(2) Facilities necessary to conduct education and research and perform related affairs may be provided to a non-tenure-track faculty member.Article 13 (Detailed Matters)Matters that are not specified in these Regulations shall be determined by the President after deliberation by the Faculty Personnel Committee.CHAPTER II ADJUNCT FACULTY MEMBERSArticle 14 (Institution to Which Adjunct Faculty Member Belongs and Classification)(1) An adjunct faculty member shall belong to a department or faculty of a college or graduate school or the College of Liberal Studies.(2) Adjunct faculty members shall be classified into adjunct professors, adjunct associate professors, and adjunct assistant professors, and Article 14 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines shall apply mutatis mutandis to the most basic career experience required for each official title.Article 15 (Qualifications)An adjunct faculty member shall meet all of the following qualifications:1. He or she shall, in principle, hold a doctorate, but this may not apply to a person who meets the qualification standards for assistant professors or above under Article 2 of the Regulations on the Standards for Qualifications of University Faculty Members;2. He or she shall be working for a Korean or foreign state agency, educational institution, research institute, public organization, industrial enterprise, etc. on a regular basis (or he or she shall have at least three years of similar career experience).Article 16 (Duties)(1) The duties of an adjunct faculty member shall be as follows:1. Lecturing on a subject requiring practical field experience in an industrial enterprise, etc., such as practical affairs, experiments, and practical skills, rather than purely academic theory;2. Providing guidance to undergraduate or graduate students on research;3. Conducting collaborative research, etc. with full-time faculty members of Seoul National University.(2) In principle, the teaching hours of an adjunct faculty member shall be not more than 9 hours each week on the basis of 15 weeks per semester. However, the teaching hours may be otherwise determined within the extent not exceeding 12 hours each week if deemed particularly necessary by the President.(3) An adjunct faculty member shall provide his or her service in consultation with the institution to which he or she originally belongs and in accordance with a separate contract for appointment.(4) An adjunct faculty member shall be held responsible only for duties he or she performs at Seoul National University.Article 17 (Treatment and Financial Resources)No remuneration shall be, in principle, paid to an adjunct faculty member. However, necessary expenses and allowances may be paid to him or her within the budget of the institution to which he or she belongs.CHAPTER III VISITING FACULTY MEMBERSArticle 18 (Institution to Which visiting Faculty Member Belongs and Classification)(1) An visiting faculty member shall belong to a department or faculty of a college or graduate school or the College of Liberal Studies.(2) Visiting faculty members shall be classified into visiting professors, invited associate professors, and invited assistant professors, and Article 14 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines shall apply mutatis mutandis to the most basic career experience required for each official title.Article 19 (Qualification)An visiting faculty member shall hold a doctorate. However, this stipulation may not apply to a person appointed to lecture on a specific subject, who meets the qualification standards for assistant professors or above under Article 2 of the Regulations on the Standards for Qualifications of University Faculty Members or has equivalent career experience in any relevant field.Article 20 (Duties)(1) The duties of an visiting faculty member shall be as follows:1. Providing lectures and practical training;2. Giving special lectures and seminars;3. Providing guidance to undergraduate or graduate students on research;4. Conducting collaborative research with full-time faculty members of Seoul National University;5. Conducting any other research designated by the head of the institution to which he or she belongs.(2) In principle, the teaching hours of an visiting faculty member shall be not more than 9 hours each week on the basis of 15 weeks per semester. However, teaching hours may be otherwise determined within the extent not exceeding 12 hours each week if deemed particularly necessary by the President.Article 21 (Treatment and Financial Resources)The treatment of an visiting faculty member shall meet all of the following requirements, and financial resources for this purpose shall be the budget of the institution to which he or she belongs:1. He or she shall be paid remuneration at a fixed amount each month;2. In accordance with the National Health Insurance Act, he or she shall become an employee insured by Seoul National University;3. The payment of retirement allowances shall be specified in his or her appointment contract or in his or her employment conditions.CHAPTER IV TEACHING FACULTY MEMBERSArticle 22 (Institution to Which Teaching Faculty Member Belongs and Classification)(1) A teaching faculty member shall belong to the Faculty of Liberal Education.(2) Teaching faculty members shall be classified into teaching professors, teaching associate professors, and teaching assistant professors, and Article 14 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines shall apply mutatis mutandis to the most basic career experience required for each official title.Article 23 (Qualification)A teaching faculty member shall hold a doctorate. However, this stipulation may not apply to a person appointed to lecture on a specific subject, who meets the qualification standards for assistant professors or above under Article 2 of the Regulations on the Standards for Qualifications of University Faculty Members or has equivalent career experience in any relevant field.Article 24 (Duties)(1) A teaching faculty member shall be in charge of 12 hours of lectures each week or equivalent lectures on the basis of 15 weeks per semester and shall engage in duties related to the development of lectures and academic affairs.(2) The details of duties related to the development of lectures and academic affairs under paragraph (1) shall be separately determined by the head of the institution to which the relevant teaching faculty member belongs within the scope that does not overlap with that of the duties of a full-time faculty member.Article 25 (Treatment and Financial Resources)The treatment of a teaching faculty member shall meet all of the following requirements, and financial resources for this purpose shall be the budget of the institution to which he or she belongs:1. He or she shall be paid remuneration at a fixed amount each month;2. In accordance with the National Health Insurance Act, he or she shall become an employee insured by Seoul National University;3. The payment of retirement allowances shall be specified in his or her appointment contract or in his or her employment conditions.CHAPTER V INVITED FACULTY MEMBERSArticle 26 (Institution to Which Special Service Faculty Member Belongs and Classification)(1) An invited faculty member shall belong to a department or faculty of a college or graduate school or the College of Liberal Studies.(2) Invited faculty members shall be classified into invited professors, invited associate professors, and invited assistant professors, and Article 14 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines shall apply mutatis mutandis to the most basic career experience required for each official title.Article 27 (Qualification)An invited faculty member shall hold a doctorate. However, this stipulation may not apply where a person appointed to lecture on a specific subject, who meets the qualification standards for assistant professors or above under Article 2 of the Regulations on the Standards for Qualifications of University Faculty Members or has equivalent career experience in any relevant field, falls under any of the following:1. A person who worked or works for a state agency, educational institution, research institute, public organization, industrial enterprise, etc.;2. A person who is a non-Korean national or resides in a foreign country and who worked or works for an educational institution or research institute.Article 28 (Duties)(1) The duties of an invited faculty member shall be as follows:1. Providing lectures and practical training;2. Giving special lectures and seminars;3. Providing guidance to undergraduate or graduate students on research;4. Conducting collaborative research with full-time faculty members of Seoul National University;5. Conducting any other research designated by the head of the institution to which he or she belongs.(2) In principle, the teaching hours of an invited faculty member shall be not more than 6 hours each week on the basis of 15 weeks per semester. However, teaching hours may be otherwise determined within the extent not exceeding 9 hours each week if deemed particularly necessary by the President.Article 29 (Treatment and Financial Resources)No remuneration shall be, in principle, paid to an invited faculty member. However, necessary expenses and allowances may be paid to him or her within the budget of the institution to which he or she belongs.CHAPTER VI CLINICAL FACULTY MEMBERSArticle 30 (Institution to Which Clinical Faculty Member Belongs and Classification)(1) A clinical faculty member shall belong to a department or faculty of a college or graduate school. However, he or she may belong to an affiliated facility, if deemed necessary.(2) Clinical faculty members shall be classified into clinical professors, clinical associate professors, and clinical assistant professors, and Article 14 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines shall apply mutatis mutandis to the most basic career experience required for each official title.Article 31 (Qualification)A clinical faculty member shall meet all of the following qualifications:1. He or she shall, in principle, hold a doctorate, but this may not apply to a person appointed to lecture on a specific subject, who meets the qualification standards for assistant professors or above under Article 2 of the Regulations on the Standards for Qualifications of University Faculty Members or has equivalent practical experience in the relevant clinical field;2. He or she shall hold a license (doctor, dentist, nurse, veterinarian, pharmacist, attorney-at-law, nutritionist, plant therapist, etc.) as an expert in the clinical field.Article 32 (Duties)(1) The duties of a clinical faculty member shall be as follows:1. Providing lectures and clinical training;2. Providing in-service education;3. Providing guidance to undergraduate or graduate students on research;4. Conducting clinical research;5. Providing clinical treatment or clinical pharmaceutical care;6. Performing any other duties designated by the head of the institution to which he or she belongs in relation to clinical education and research.(2) In principle, the teaching hours of a clinical faculty member shall be not more than 6 hours each week on the basis of 15 weeks per semester.However, the teaching hours may be otherwise determined within the extent not exceeding 9 hours each week if deemed particularly necessary by the President.Article 33 (Treatment and Financial Resources)(1) Remuneration shall be paid to a full-time clinical faculty member by a separate contract, and financial resources for this purpose shall be the budget of the institution to which he or she belongs.(2) No remuneration shall be, in principle, paid to a part-time clinical faculty member. However, necessary expenses and allowances may be paid to him or her within the budget of the institution to which he or she belongs.CHAPTER VII INDUSTRY-ACADEMIA COOPERATION FACULTY MEMBERSArticle 34 (Institution to Which Industry-Academia Cooperation Faculty Member Belongs)An industry-academia cooperation faculty member shall belong to a department or faculty of a college or graduate school, the College of Liberal Studies, a research facility, or the Industry-Academia Cooperation Foundation.Article 35 (Qualifications)An industry-academia cooperation faculty member shall meet all of the following qualifications:1. He or she shall, in principle, hold a doctorate, but this may not apply to a person appointed to lecture on a specific subject, who meets the qualification standards for assistant professors or above under Article 2 of the Regulations on the Standards for Qualifications of University Faculty Members or has equivalent career experience in any relevant field;2. He or she shall have at least ten years’ experience working for a private industrial enterprise, state agency, or institution equivalent to a state agency (a public institution or public organization) in relation to the field of his or her specialty.Article 36 (Duties)(1) The duties of an industry-academia cooperation faculty member shall be as follows:1. Providing lectures and conducting research activities;2. Human resources training based on demand by industrial enterprises and future industrial development;3. Conducting research, development, and commercialization activities for the creation and dissemination of new knowledge and technologies;4. Collaboratively using tangible and intangible resources held, such as human resources, facilities, equipment, and information on research and development.(2) In principle, the teaching hours of an industry-academia cooperation faculty member shall be not more than 6 hours each week on the basis of 15 weeks per semester. However, the teaching hours may be otherwise determined within the extent not exceeding 9 hours each week if deemed particularly necessary by the President.Article 37 (Treatment and Financial Resources)Remuneration shall be paid to an industry-academia cooperation faculty member by a separate contract, and financial resources for that purpose shall be the budget of the institution to which he or she belongs.CHAPTER VIII GUEST FACULTY MEMBERSArticle 38 (Institution to Which Guest Faculty Member Belongs and Classification)(1) A Guest faculty member shall belong to a department or faculty of a college or graduate school or the College of Liberal Studies. However, he or she may belong to an affiliated facility or an institution approved by the President, if deemed necessary.(2) Guest faculty members shall be classified into guest professors, visiting associate professors, and visiting assistant professors, and Article 14 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines shall apply mutatis mutandis to the most basic career experience required for each official title.Article 39 (Qualification)A guest faculty member shall hold a doctorate. However, this may not apply to a person appointed to lecture on a specific subject, who meets the qualification standards for assistant professors or above under Article 2 of the Regulations on the Standards for Qualifications of University Faculty Members or has equivalent career experience in any relevant field.Article 40 (Duties)(1) The duties of a guest faculty member shall be as follows:1. Providing lectures and practical training;2. Giving special lectures and seminars;3. Providing guidance to undergraduate or graduate students on research;4. Conducting collaborative research with full-time faculty members of Seoul National University.(2) In principle, the teaching hours of a guest faculty member shall be not more than 6 hours each week on the basis of 15 weeks per semester. However, teaching hours may be otherwise determined within the extent not exceeding 9 hours each week if deemed particularly necessary by the President.Article 41 (Treatment and Financial Resources)(1) Remuneration shall be paid to a full-time guest faculty member by a separate contract, and financial resources for that purpose shall be the budget of the institution to which he or she belongs.(2) No remuneration shall be, in principle, paid to a part-time guest faculty member. However, necessary expenses and allowances may be paid to him or her within the budget of the institution to which he or she belongs.CHAPTER IX CLINICAL INSTRUCTORSArticle 42 (Institution to Which Clinical Instructor Belongs)A clinical instructor shall belong to a department or faculty of a college or graduate school. However, he or she may belong to an affiliated facility, if deemed necessary.Article 43 (Qualification)A clinical instructor shall meet all of the following qualifications:1. He or she shall, in principle, hold a doctorate, but this may not apply to a person appointed to lecture on a specific subject, who meets the qualification standards for assistant professors or above under Article 2 of the Regulations on the Standards for Qualifications of University Faculty Members or has equivalent practical experience in the relevant clinical field;2. He or she shall hold a license (doctor, dentist, nurse, veterinarian, pharmacist, attorney-at-law, nutritionist, plant therapist, etc.) as an expert in the clinical field.Article 44 (Duties)(1) The duties of a clinical instructor shall be as follows:1. Providing lectures and practical training;2. Providing guidance to undergraduate or graduate students on research and conducting other activities.(2) In principle, the teaching hours of a clinical instructor shall be not more than 6 hours each week on the basis of 15 weeks per semester. However, teaching hours may be otherwise determined within the extent not exceeding 9 hours each week if deemed particularly necessary by the President.Article 45 (Treatment and Financial Resources)No remuneration shall be, in principle, paid to a clinical instructor. However, necessary expenses and allowances may be paid to him or her within the budget of the institution to which he or she belongs.CHAPTER X RESEARCH FACULTY MEMBERSArticle 46 (Institution to Which Research Faculty Member Belongs and Classification)(1) A research faculty member shall belong to a research facility or an institution deemed necessary by the President.(2) Research faculty members shall be classified into research professors, research associate professors, and research assistant professors, and Article 14 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines shall apply mutatis mutandis to the most basic career experience required for each official title.Article 47 (Qualification)A research faculty member shall hold a doctorate. However, this stipulation may not apply to a person who meets the qualification standards for assistant professors or above under Article 2 of the Regulations on the Standards for Qualifications of University Faculty Members or has equivalent career experience in any relevant field.Article 48 (Duties)The duties of a research faculty member shall be as follows:1. Performing research tasks designated by the head of the institution to which he or she belongs;2. Conducting collaborative research with full-time faculty members of Seoul National University;3. Conducting departmental affairs designated by the head of the institution to which he or she belongs.Article 49 (Treatment and Financial Resources)A research faculty member belonging to a research facility shall be paid remuneration determined by the head of the institution to which he or she belongs within the budget of the institution. However, a research faculty member belonging to an institution other than a research facility may be paid remuneration from separate financial resources if deemed necessary by the President.Article 50 (BK Faculty Members)The regulations relating to the Brain Korea (BK) 21 Project shall apply to a BK faculty member who is appointed through screening by each project team in order to be devoted to education and research within BK 21 project teams in accordance with the BK 21 Project.CHAPTER XI SUPPLEMENTARY PROVISIONSArticle 51 (Detailed Guidelines for Each Institution)Detailed guidelines concerning the appointment, service, etc. of a non-tenure-track faculty member shall be separately determined by the head of the institution to which he or she belongs after deliberation by the personnel committee (or steering committee for personnel management) of the institution. However, where an affiliated facility or research facility supervised by a college or graduate school intends to determine detailed guidelines concerning the appointment, service, etc. of a non-tenure-track faculty member, it shall obtain approval from the dean of the supervising college or graduate school.ADDENDAArticle 1 (Enforcement Date)These Regulations shall enter into force on August 1, 2019.Article 2 (Applicability)These Regulations shall apply, beginning with non-tenure-track faculty members newly appointed on or after August 1, 2019 (including the procedure for newly employing relevant non-tenure-track faculty members).
