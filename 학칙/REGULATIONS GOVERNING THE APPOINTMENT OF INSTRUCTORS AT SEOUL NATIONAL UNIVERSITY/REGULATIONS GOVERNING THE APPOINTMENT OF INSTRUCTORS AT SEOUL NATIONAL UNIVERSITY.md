# REGULATIONS GOVERNING THE APPOINTMENT OF INSTRUCTORS AT SEOUL NATIONAL UNIVERSITY
> 학교규정 · 학칙 · 시행 2019-06-12 · 공포 2019-06-12 · 일부개정

### 전문
CHAPTER I: GENERAL PROVISIONSArticle 1 (Purpose)The purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe matters concerning the employment of instructors at Seoul National University pursuant to Article 3 (3) of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines of Seoul National University (hereinafter referred to as the “Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines”).Article 2 (Scope of Application)These Regulations shall apply to instructors under Article 2 (3) of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines.Article 3 (Qualifications)(1) An instructor shall be a person who holds a doctorate. However, the foregoing shall not apply where he or she falls under any of the following:1. A person who holds a degree at least equivalent to a bachelor’s degree and has at least three years' experience in teaching and research;2. A person who is an authority in the relevant field and has at least ten years' experience in teaching and research.(2) Where, in consideration of the characteristics of the relevant subject, it is difficult to employ a person who has experience in teaching and research, a person who falls under any subparagraph of Article 11 of the Regulations on the Standards for Qualifications of University Faculty Members, but not falling under paragraph (1) of this Article, may be employed as an instructor after deliberation by the Faculty Personnel Committee. <Amended by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>Article 4 (Institution to Which the Instructor Belongs and Subjects on Which the Instructor Shall Provide Lectures)(1) An instructor shall belong to a department or faculty of a college or graduate school, the College of Liberal Studies, or the Faculty of Liberal Education and provide lectures on the subjects offered by the relevant college or graduate school, the College of Liberal Studies, the Faculty of Liberal Education, etc. (hereinafter referred to as “college, graduate school, etc.”). However, the President may, if deemed necessary, assign an instructor to a college or graduate school. <Amended by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>(2) Notwithstanding paragraph (1), if the Dean of a College, Graduate School, etc. to which an instructor belongs (hereinafter referred to as the “head of the institution”) deems necessary, an instructor may provide lectures on a subject offered by any other College, Graduate School, etc. of Seoul National University, after obtaining approval from the head of the institution.Article 5 (Appointment)(1) An instructor shall be appointed by the President after deliberation by the Faculty Personnel Committee on the recommendation of the head of the institution.(2) The appointment of instructors shall be made biannually, on March 1 and September 1 every year, and the period of employment shall be at least one year. However, an instructor may be employed for less than one year and his or her appointment may be made on any date other than March 1 or September 1 where a substitute instructor is urgently necessary for the remainder of a semester due to less than six months of sick leave, maternity leave, leave of absence, dispatch of a faculty member, disciplinary action against a faculty member, not more than six months of a faculty member’s research leave, or release from position, retirement, or dismissal of a faculty member, in the middle of such semester.(3) In appointing a person meeting the qualification prescribed in Article 3 as an instructor, if deemed necessary to foster subsequent academic generations, an instructor may be appointed by differently determining the scope of persons eligible to apply and criteria for examining them, and the detailed subcriteria shall be separately determined following deliberation by the personnel committee of the relevant College, Graduate School, etc. <Newly Inserted by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>(4) An instructor shall be guaranteed procedures for reappointment for a total period of three years including the period of new employment, and procedures for new employment or reappointment shall proceed thereafter.Article 6 (Contract for Appointment)(1) The President shall appoint an instructor by prescribing the terms and conditions of work, including the following matters:1. Period of appointment and hours of lectures;2. Wages and method of payment;3. Terms and conditions of work, such as service;4. Grounds for dismissal;5. Conditions and procedures for reappointment;6. Other matters necessary to be prescribed by contract with respect to the appointment and service of instructors.(2) A contract for appointment under paragraph (1) shall be concluded in writing, and shall, in principle, be prepared according to the attached Form.(3) The period of appointment under paragraph (1) 1 shall also include a period during which an instructor does not provide lectures in the middle of the period of appointment due to non-offering of his or her subjects, because such subjects are offered only in a semester when it is necessary to offer them, due to the impossibility of continuously offering them for any cause related to the operation of the curriculum, such as subjects not offered every semester, seasonal classes, and the operation of on-the-job training classes.Article 7 (Lecture Hours)In principle, the lecture hours of an instructor shall be not more than 6 hours a week based on 30 weeks an academic year. However, in any of the following cases, lecture hours may be differently determined within the extent not exceeding 9 hours a week:1. In cases of providing lectures on any subject offered by any other college or undergraduate school of Seoul National University pursuant to Article 4 (2);2. In cases of providing lectures on any subject requiring guidance of practical skill under the proviso to Article 3 (1) of the Regulations Governing the Payment of Fees for Seoul National University Lectures.<Newly Inserted by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>3. Other cases deemed particularly necessary by the President.Article 8 (Personnel Records)The President shall prepare, maintain, and keep personnel records for the efficient personnel management of instructors belonging to Seoul National University.CHAPTER II: APPOINTMENTSECTION 1: NEW APPOINTMENTArticle 9 (Principle of Open Employment)The new employment of instructors shall be, in principle, based on open employment, but an instructor may be specially employed where he or she is appointed for less than one year pursuant to the proviso to Article 5 (2).Article 10 (Procedures for New Employment)(1) Procedures for the new employment of instructors shall proceed in the following order: public announcement, receipt of applications, screening, selection, recommendation, and appointment.(2) The head of the institution who intends to openly employ an instructor shall publicly announce matters concerning the field of employment, the number of persons to be employed, qualification for application, screening criteria, and other matters for at least seven days in an effective manner such as publication on the website of the relevant institution. However, where there is no applicant or eligible person, although open employment has been conducted, procedures for open employment may be repeated, by fixing a public announcement period of at least five days. <Amended by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>(3) In cases of specially employing an instructor pursuant to Article 9, the procedures prescribed in paragraph (1) may be partially omitted.Article 11 (Screening of Candidates for New Employment)(1) The screening of candidates for new employment shall be conducted in the following phases, and, if necessary, such screening may be conducted by integrating all phases or omitting interview screening, within the extent not impairing fairness and objectivity of screening. Detailed matters necessary for conducting such screening shall be separately determined by the head of the institution: <Amended by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>1. The Screening of Application Documents:(a) Fitness for the field of study (subject) recruited for;(b) Educational career and overall research achievements;(c) A letter of self-introduction (personal statement) and education plan;2. Interview screening: fitness for appointment and other matters.(2) With respect to outcomes in the arts and athletic circles or any special field (design, planning, etc.) contained in the list of overall research achievements under paragraph (1) 1 (b), such outcomes entered in that list may be required to be presented.(3) The detailed procedures, and the items, weights, methods, etc. of detailed evaluation, which are necessary for evaluating the matters under each subparagraph of paragraph (1), shall be separately prescribed by the head of the institution in consideration of the necessary academic characteristics, after deliberation by the personnel committee of the relevant college, graduate school, etc.(4) In principle, persons eligible for interview screening shall be selected within not more than three times the number of people to be actually recruited in each field, according to the result of application screening.Article 12 (Screening Committee Members)(1) For the purpose of conducting employment screening under Article 11 (1), the head of the institution may commission or appoint as screening committee members three or more persons from among full-time faculty members related to the field of the relevant study (subject) or relevant experts with sufficient knowledge and experience, and the screening committee members for each phase may be composed of the same persons.(2) A person who has difficulty maintaining fairness in screening shall not be commissioned or appointed as a screening committee member. However, in extenuating circumstances, the foregoing provisions may be otherwise applied after deliberation by the personnel committee of the relevant college of graduate school, etc.Article 13 (Screening Methods)(1) Each screening committee member shall evaluate based on the items and weights determined pursuant to Article 11, and where screening committee members are five or more persons, the acquired marks for an item shall be the average of the evaluation marks remaining after excluding the highest and lowest evaluation marks.(2) The total evaluation marks shall be calculated by adding up the acquired marks by item under paragraph (1), and the candidate for employment may be determined in the order of the highest marks. If there are ties, their order shall be determined according to the criteria prescribed by the personnel committee of the relevant college, graduate school, etc.Article 14 (Submission of Screening Results and Recommendation of Candidate for Employment)(1) After completing the screening process, the head of the institution shall submit the applications and screening results of all screened candidates to the President.(2) The head of the institution shall determine the candidate for employment after deliberation by the personnel committee of the relevant college, graduate school, etc. and submit to the President a report on candidate’s screening for employment as an instructor, in which the period of appointment is entered.(3) Where the candidate for employment abandons the appointment prior to the scheduled date of appointment, the person with the next highest score may be appointed as an instructor. <Newly Inserted by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>Article 15 (Required Documents)(1) When the head of the institution recommends the appointment of an instructor, he or she shall submit the following documents:1. A personal questionnaire;2. A personnel record card;3. A graduation certificate and diploma;4. A career certificate;5. A written reply to an inquiry into sex offense history;6. Minutes of the meeting of the personnel committee of the relevant college, graduate school, etc.;7. A copy of a visa (limited to non-Korean nationals) meeting criteria for stay issued pursuant to the Immigration Act after obtaining approval from the President;8. Other documents deemed necessary for appointment by the President.(2) Where any defect is discovered in any document submitted in connection with paragraph (1), or any of the aforesaid documents is deemed false, the President shall require the candidate for appointment to supplement or resubmit such documents within a reasonable fixed period, and where the candidate for new employment fails to take appropriate measures, such as supplementation of such documents, the President may cancel the decision on his or her appointment.Article 16 (Grounds for Disqualification of Appointment)No person who falls under any subparagraph of Article 19 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines shall be appointed as an instructor.SECTION 2: REAPPOINTMENTArticle 17 (Timing of Reappointment)Reappointments of instructors shall be made biannually: on March 1 and September 1 each year.Article 18 (Provision of Opportunity, and Procedure, for Reappointment)(1) The standards and procedures prescribed in Article 19 shall apply to the reappointment of an instructor (excluding, however, an instructor appointed for less than one year pursuant to the proviso to Article 5 (2)) whose period of appointment expires.(2) The head of the institution shall notify the relevant instructor whose period of appointment expires in writing of the fact that his or her period of appointment will expire, and ask whether the instructor is able to file an application for screening for reappointment no later than four months before such appointment expires.(3) Where the head of the institution deems that the relevant instructor fails to meet the standards for reappointment established pursuant to paragraph (1), the head shall provide the instructor with an opportunity to provide explanations, such as by presentation of his or her opinions. In such cases, the head shall notify the instructor of the time and place for presenting his or her opinion, etc., no later than 15 days in advance, and allow the instructor to make explanations, such as by presentation of his or her opinion, according to the methods and procedures separately determined by the personnel committee of the relevant college, graduate school, etc. Such explanations shall be reflected in the screening reappointment.(4) The Faculty Personnel Committee shall provide an instructor determined as failing to meet the standards based on the results of screening for reappointment with an opportunity to provide explanations by requesting the instructor to prepare and submit a detailed statement or to attend its meeting and make a statement.(5) Where the President determines not to reappoint an instructor after deliberation by the Faculty Personnel Committee, he or she shall notify the relevant instructor of such a fact, specifying the intention not to reappoint that instructor and detailed reasons for such a decision in writing no later than one month prior to the expiration of appointment period.(6) Where an instructor whose reappointment is rejected intends to object to the decision, he or she may file a request for examination with the Teacher’s Appeal Examination Committee under Article 7 of the Special Act on the Improvement of the Status of Teachers and the Protection of Their Educational Activities, within 30 days from the date he or she becomes aware of the decision.Article 19 (Screening for Reappointment)(1) With respect to the reappointment of an instructor, the following matters regarding achievements made during the relevant period of appointment shall be screened objectively. However, the period during which the instructor fails to provide lectures due to the non-offering of lectures during his or her appointment period shall be excluded from the period subject to screening for reappointment:1. Educational activities (evaluations on lectures, operation of lectures, and other activities related to education): 80 marks;2. Evaluation by the head of the institution (concerning compliance with the education-related statutes and regulations, dignity as an instructor, contributions to the college, graduate school, etc.): 20 marks;3. Additional marks may be given in cases of receiving a prize, decoration, or official citation: 5 marks.(2) Where an instructor receives 70 or more marks as a result of screening for reappointment, the head of the institution shall deem that instructor eligible for reappointment.(3) The detailed procedures, items, weights, and methods for evaluation which are necessary for evaluating the matters under each subparagraph of paragraph (1) shall be separately determined by the head of the institution in consideration of the necessary academic characteristics, after deliberation by the personnel committee of the relevant college, graduate school, etc.(4) Where it is impractical to conduct the screening under paragraph (1) due to an unavoidable reason, such as for subjects that do not require lecture evaluation or team teaching, the relevant college, graduate school, etc. may conduct the screening after establishing its own standards after deliberation by its personnel committee.(5) The head of the institution shall determine whether to reappoint an instructor no later than two months prior to the date on which such instructor’s period of appointment expires, after deliberation by the personnel committee of the relevant college, graduate school, etc., and submit the screening results of such an instructor to the President.(6) Articles 12 and 13 shall apply to other matters concerning the composition of a screening committee, and the methods of screening.Article 20 (Determination, etc. of An Expected Reappointee)Where the President deems that a candidate for reappointment recommended by the head of the institution is an eligible person, he or she shall determine such a candidate as an expected reappointee after deliberation by the Faculty Personnel Committee. However, if the candidate for reappointment is found to be significantly ineligible to be an instructor because he or she falls under any of the following cases, the President may refuse to reappoint such an instructor after deliberation by the Faculty Personnel Committee:1. Where he or she has aroused social controversy or seriously degraded his or her reputation as a faculty member;2. Where significant misconduct related to his or her research process or research expenses management has been revealed;3. Where he or she has significantly undermined research ethics, such as by committing research practice misconduct.CHAPTER III: DUTIES AND GUARANTEE OF STATUSArticle 21 (Duties of Instructor)An instructor shall faithfully provide lectures, prepare and provide public notice of the lectures and corresponding syllabi for such lectures, evaluate and input the grades of their students, and develop teaching materials and methods.Article 22 (Mandatory Retirement)Where an instructor falls under any of the following, he or she shall mandatorily retire:1. Where he or she dies;2. Where he or she reaches the age of 65 (in such a case, his or her period of appointment shall expire on the last day of the semester in which he or she reaches the age of 65; <Amended by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>3. Where he or she falls under Article 19 of the Regulations Governing Faculty Policies, Procedures, Guidelines. However, subparagraph 2 of the same Article shall apply only where a person declared bankrupt fails to file an application for immunity within an application deadline pursuant to Debtor Rehabilitation and Bankruptcy Act, or he or she is a person in whose case either a decision not to grant immunity or the revocation of immunity becomes final and decisive pursuant to the same Act; and subparagraph 5 of that Article shall apply only where he or she is a person under a suspended sentence of imprisonment without labor or a heavier punishment as declared by a court due to committing a crime provided for in Articles 129 through 132 or Article 303 of the Criminal Act or Article 10 of the Act on Special Cases concerning the Punishment of Sexual Crimes or a crime provided for in Article 355 or 356 of the Criminal Act in relation to his or her job duties.Article 23 (Dismissal of Instructor)(1) Where an instructor falls under any of the following subparagraphs, the President may dismiss him or her following deliberation by the Faculty Personnel Committee, on the recommendation of the head of the institution: <Amended by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>1. Where he or she violates or fails to faithfully perform duties stated in an employment contract;2. Where he or she arouses social controversy or seriously degrades his or her reputation as a faculty member;3. Where significant misconduct related to his or her research process or research expenses management is revealed;4. Where he or she significantly undermines research ethics, such as committing unethical research practice;5. Where his or her dismissal is deemed unavoidable due to such reasons as the abolition of the relevant department.6. Where he or she becomes unable to perform his or her duties due to illness or any other reason.(2) Where an instructor is deemed to fall under any subparagraph of paragraph (1), the Faculty Personnel Committee shall provide such an instructor with opportunities to provide explanations. <Newly Inserted by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>Article 24 (Retirement Age) Deleted: <by Regulations No. 2160, Jun. 12, 2019>CHAPTER IV: REMUNERATION AND SERVICEArticle 25 (Remuneration of Instructor)(1) With respect to the matter concerning fees for lectures and wages during vacations paid to instructors, the Regulations Governing Payment of Fees for Lectures at Seoul National University shall apply.(2) Other necessary matters concerning the payment of fees for lectures may be separately prescribed by the President.Article 26 (Service of Instructor)(1) Where an instructor intends to hold a concurrent post at any institution other than Seoul National University, he or she shall make a prior report to the head of the institution to which he or she belongs.(2) An instructor shall comply with the matters prescribed by education-related statutes and regulations.(3) Other matters concerning service not prescribed by these Regulations shall be separately determined by the head of the institution, and the instructor shall comply with such matters.CHAPTER V: SUPPLEMENTARY PROVISIONSArticle 27 (Detailed Guidelines of Each Institution)Detailed guidelines concerning the appointment, service, etc. of instructors shall be separately determined by the head of theinstitution after deliberation by the personnel committee of each college, graduate school, etc., but shall undergo deliberation by the Faculty Personnel Committee and require approval from the President.Article 28 (Other Matters)Other matters not prescribed by these Regulations may be separately determined and implemented by the President.
