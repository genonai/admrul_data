1. 항공통상우편요금 [[별표 1](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/admRulBylInfoPLinkR.do?admRulSeq=2100000281734&bylNo=0001&bylBrNo=00)과 같다] 2. 선편통상우편요금 [[별표 2](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/admRulBylInfoPLinkR.do?admRulSeq=2100000281734&bylNo=0002&bylBrNo=00)와 같다] 3. 소포우편요금 [[별표 3](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/admRulBylInfoPLinkR.do?admRulSeq=2100000281734&bylNo=0003&bylBrNo=00)과 같다] 4.
